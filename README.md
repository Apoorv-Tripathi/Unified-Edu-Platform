# 🎓 Unified Education Interface (UEI)
## Smart India Hackathon 2025 – Problem Statement ID: 1525

A next-generation AI-powered education ecosystem designed to unify students, faculty, institutions, and administrative intelligence into a single scalable digital platform.

---

# 🚀 Project Overview

Unified Education Interface (UEI) is a fully developed education management and analytics platform built to solve the problem of fragmented academic systems in India.

The platform centralizes:
- Student academic records
- Faculty performance
- Institutional analytics
- AI-powered insights
- Administrative decision support

into one modern, intelligent, and scalable ecosystem.

Unlike traditional educational portals, UEI integrates advanced analytics and AI capabilities to help administrators, teachers, and institutions make data-driven decisions.

The platform is designed with scalability, accessibility, and digital transformation in mind and aligns with the vision of Digital India and NEP 2020.
